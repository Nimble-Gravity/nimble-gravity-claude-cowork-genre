# About me

## Role
Senior Analytics Engineer, Global IT — Enterprise Data & AI Services, Gen Re. I report to the
Director, Data & Platform Engineering.

## Accountable for
Modernizing the legacy model estate — turning undocumented Excel/VBA and R models into
documentation and build-ready specs the Python platform team can implement.

## Where my week goes
- Writing process documentation and specs from legacy Excel/VBA and R models
- Reviewing code — mine and the team's — before it ships to the platform
- Interviewing model owners to capture the business rules the code doesn't explain
- Keeping the modernization backlog and stand-up notes current
- Answering the platform team's questions about legacy model behavior

## Tools & data
- Microsoft 365 — Outlook, Teams, SharePoint (documentation standards and specs live in SharePoint)
- The legacy model estate (Excel/VBA workbooks, R scripts, SQL) and the Python platform repo
- VS Code, Git, and Excel for tracing legacy logic

## What "good" looks like
Documentation a new analyst could follow without me in the room: every claim cites the line or
cell it came from, every defect is flagged with what could go wrong, and everything the source
doesn't explain is an open question — not a guess. Rushed work is generic, uncited, and glosses
over what it doesn't know.

## Approval chain
I draft → the Director, Data & Platform Engineering reviews → the platform team builds from it.
My work is always a draft the responsible engineer signs off on, never the final word.
